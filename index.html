The shop

A factory for fades in Brooklyn's oldest neighborhood

D'Fades Factory opened on Stillwell Ave in 2023 and earned a perfect five stars doing one thing: treating every head like it matters. It's a full crew of barbers — Dominican craft, every texture, classic to modern — with music on, kids welcome, and no attitude at the door.

Gravesend has been here since 1643. The fades are newer.
