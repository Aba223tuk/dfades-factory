The dining room

A warm, low-lit room for dinner and cocktails — lamb chops off the grill, lobster mac, a filet-mignon burger, and dessert worth staying for. Date-night volume: music on, conversation intact.

Hookah with clean-burning Quasar tops, cocktails, the flower wall, and private rooms for celebrations. DJs spin Armenian, Middle Eastern and hip-hop late into the night.
