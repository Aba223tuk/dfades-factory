## Competitive position

Nearest rival (House of Fadez, 2315 Stillwell) has no website. Neighborhood leaders
(Mensch, Kingsmen) have site + Booksy. A real site with published menu and a booking flow
moves D'Fades from "nothing online but IG" to ahead of its entire block.
